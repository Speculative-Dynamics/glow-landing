4-Band Studio

Control each of the 4 horizontal lighting sections independently with smooth gradient blending, HSB color wheel, and exact hex inputs.

Siri & Shortcuts

Built on Apple's modern AppIntents framework. Speak naturally to Siri or automate lighting routines using the iOS Shortcuts app.

🛡️

100% Offline & Private

Zero telemetry, zero user tracking, and no external network traffic. All Bluetooth commands stay strictly within your room.

Native Siri Voice Control

Glowmancer registers directly with Apple Siri out-of-the-box. Try speaking these phrases to your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch:

"Hey Siri, turn on Glowmancer"
"Hey Siri, turn off Glowmancer"
"Hey Siri, set Glowmancer to Warm White"
"Hey Siri, set Glowmancer to Cozy"
"Hey Siri, set Glowmancer to Red"
"Hey Siri, turn Glowmancer Blue"
"Hey Siri, play Meteor on Glowmancer"
"Hey Siri, set Glowmancer brightness to 80%"
