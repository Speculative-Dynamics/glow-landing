Support

Glow is a direct Bluetooth remote for compatible lamps. It does not require an account, Wi-Fi, or a cloud service.

Get in touch

For pairing help, a compatibility question, or a problem with Glow, email support. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Email Glow Support Report an issue on GitHub

Pairing and reconnecting

  • Keep the lamp powered on and your iPhone within Bluetooth range.
  • Allow Bluetooth access when Glow asks. You can change this later in iPhone Settings.
  • Open Glow and wait for the connection status to show Connected.
  • If the lamp does not respond, open Connection in Glow, choose Reconnect, then try again nearby.

Privacy questions

Glow does not collect or transmit personal data. Preferences remain on your iPhone and can be removed by deleting the app. Read the full policy for details.
